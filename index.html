Frequently Asked Questions About Manual Driving Lessons

How do I book a manual driving lesson?
To book a lesson, press on the book now button and view available times on the scheduling page. Choose your preferred date and time slot.
Do I need a valid driver's license for manual driving lessons?
Yes, a valid G2 or G license is required to take our manual driving lessons.
Are the manual driving lessons suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. We assess your current skill level before the lesson and tailor our instruction accordingly, whether you're a complete beginner or need a refresher.
Are these manual driving lessons eligible for MTO Certification?
No, these lessons only teach fully licensed individuals how to operate a manual transmission. A valid G2 or G license is required to take these lessons.
How do you process payments for manual driving lessons?
Payments are handled in-person at the start of the lesson. We accept either e-transfer or cash payments.
How do I book the 3-Lesson manual driving package?
When you click on "Book Now", the scheduling page appears. Pick a date and time, then press "Select and choose another time" and add another lesson twice to complete the package.
What is the cancellation/rescheduling policy for manual driving lessons?
For cancellations and rescheduling, please provide at least 24 hours notice. You can cancel or reschedule through the booking appointment sent to your email.

Where Can You Find Our Manual Driving Lessons?

Manual driving lessons are conducted in Mississauga, specifically at the Streetsville Go Station parking lot. This location provides the perfect environment for learning manual transmission skills.
